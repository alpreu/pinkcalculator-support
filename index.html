FAQ

How do I delete the last digit?
Swipe left or right on the number display.

How do percentages work?
With + and −, the percentage is applied to the first number (e.g. 200 + 10% = 220). With × and ÷, it converts the number to a fraction (e.g. 200 × 10% = 20).

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Pink Calculator works completely offline and never connects to the internet.
