Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Pink Calculator app for iOS, developed by Alexander Preuss.

Summary

Pink Calculator does not collect, store, or share any data whatsoever.

Data Collection

The app:

  • collects no personal information,
  • contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks,
  • makes no network connections of any kind,
  • requires no account or sign-in,
  • requests no device permissions.

All calculations happen locally on your device and are not stored after you close the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated here before that version is released.
